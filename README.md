# Immigrant Occupations

### Region groupings

Regions follow the headings in the IPUMS USA birthplace codebook ([BPL](https://usa.ipums.org/usa-action/variables/BPL#codes_section)), assigned by detailed birthplace code (`bpld`). Only birthplaces separately identified in the 2020–2024 ACS are listed.

- **United States** (`bpld` < 10000): 50 states and DC
- **US Territories** (10000–14999, 71047): Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands. Counted as native-born by the Census Bureau, so excluded from immigrant tables.
- **Other North America** (15000–19999): Canada, Bermuda, Cape Verde
- **Central America and Caribbean** (20000–29999): Mexico, Belize, Costa Rica, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Panama, Cuba, Dominican Republic, Haiti, Jamaica, Antigua and Barbuda, Bahamas, Barbados, Dominica, Grenada, St. Kitts and Nevis, St. Lucia, St. Vincent and the Grenadines, Trinidad and Tobago, Caribbean n.s., West Indies n.s., Americas n.s.
- **South America** (30000–39999): Argentina, Bolivia, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guyana, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, Venezuela, South America n.s.
- **Northern Europe** (40000–41999): Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway, Sweden, England, Scotland, United Kingdom n.s., Northern Ireland, Ireland
- **Western Europe** (42000–42999): Belgium, France, Netherlands, Switzerland
- **Southern Europe** (43000–44999): Albania, Greece, North Macedonia, Italy, Portugal (incl. Azores), Spain
- **Central/Eastern Europe** (45000–46999): Austria, Bulgaria, Czechoslovakia, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Germany, Hungary, Poland, Romania, Yugoslavia, Croatia, Montenegro, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Kosovo, Latvia, Lithuania, Russia/other USSR, Belarus, Moldova, Ukraine, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, USSR n.s.
- **Other Europe** (49900): Europe n.s. (small countries not separately identified in the ACS, e.g., Estonia, Slovenia, Luxembourg, Malta)
- **East Asia** (50000–50999): China, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Mongolia, Japan, Korea
- **Southeast Asia** (51000–51999): Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam
- **India/Southwest Asia** (52000–52999): Afghanistan, India, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Burma, Pakistan, Sri Lanka, Iran, Nepal
- **Middle East/Asia Minor** (53000–54999): Iraq, Israel/Palestine, Jordan, Kuwait, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Syria, Turkey, United Arab Emirates, Yemen
- **Other Asia** (59900): Asia n.e.c./n.s. (countries not separately identified in the ACS, e.g., Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, Brunei, Macau, Maldives)
- **Africa** (60000–69999): Algeria, Egypt, Libya, Morocco, Sudan, Gambia, Ghana, Guinea, Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Nigeria, Senegal, Sierra Leone, Togo, Ethiopia, Kenya, Rwanda, Somalia, Tanzania, Uganda, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Eritrea, Cameroon, Republic of the Congo, DR Congo, South Africa, plus North/West/East Africa n.s. and Africa n.s.
- **Oceania** (70000–79999, excl. 71047): Australia, New Zealand, Fiji, Tonga, Marshall Islands, Micronesia
- **Other/Unknown** (95000): Other n.e.c.

**Notes**
- Groupings follow IPUMS, not Census Bureau regions. Differences from Census: Burma and Iran are in India/Southwest Asia (Census: Southeast Asia and South Central Asia); the Caucasus and Central Asian former Soviet states are in Central/Eastern Europe (Census: Western Asia and South Central Asia); Cape Verde is in Other North America (Census: Western Africa).
- Several travel-ban countries have no separate ACS birthplace code and fall into "n.s." catch-alls. Examples: Chad, Mali, Niger and Burkina Faso (Africa n.s. codes) and Turkmenistan (Other Asia or USSR n.s.).
